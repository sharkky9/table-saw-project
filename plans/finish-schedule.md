# Finish Schedule

## Goal

This is shop furniture. The target is:

- good hand feel
- durable sealed surfaces
- easy cleanup
- no splintery edges

It is not a furniture-grade showpiece.

## Surface Classes

### Class A: Touch And Precision Surfaces

- fixed top
- miter-station cover
- router module
- drawer fronts and pulls zone

Treatment:

- ease edges with a `1/16 to 1/8 in` roundover or chamfer
- sand to `150` then `180`
- keep the prefinished top fields as-delivered except for light scuff cleanup if needed
- seal only the raw cut edges and any non-prefinished matching parts with satin waterborne polyurethane

### Class B: Visible Working Surfaces

- outer cabinet faces
- service doors
- support faces
- utility faces

Treatment:

- break edges
- sand to `150`
- apply one light seal coat only where a raw face or edge would otherwise stay thirsty or fuzzy

### Class C: Hidden Structure

- plinth
- inside framing
- underside blocking

Treatment:

- knock down splinters
- spot-sand hand-contact edges
- optional quick seal coat in dust-prone zones

## Do Not Finish First

Do not fully coat these until fit is proven:

- miter-station latch and stop contact pads
- router-plate ledges
- saw mount interfaces
- overlay anchor points

Mask or scrape these surfaces after finish if needed.

## Sample Board Step

Before touching the bench:

1. Prepare offcuts of prefinished plywood, standard plywood, and construction lumber.
2. Ease the edges exactly how you plan to ease the bench.
3. Test the edge-seal or touch-up finish schedule on the raw cut edges.
4. Confirm:
   - hand feel
   - sheen
   - stain resistance
   - whether the raw cut edges need a second seal coat to match the prefinished faces closely enough

## Recommended Sequence

1. Machine and dry-fit everything.
2. Pre-finish only the few interior panels or raw faces that are hard to reach later.
3. Assemble the bench.
4. Final sand exposed raw faces and edges.
5. Seal raw cut edges and any non-prefinished Class A faces.
6. Seal any remaining Class B raw faces only if needed.
7. Re-open critical fit surfaces if finish buildup tightens them.
