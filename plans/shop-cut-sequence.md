# Shop Cut Sequence

## Purpose

This is the builder-facing cut order. It does not replace the formal cut lists. It translates them into a lower-churn shop workflow.

Use this sequence when roughing stock so you minimize fence resets and repeated layout work.

## General Rules

- break down full sheets first
- group by material and thickness before switching tools or setups
- do long rips before short crosscuts
- label every part immediately after it is cut
- where two parts share the same blank size, cut them together if face orientation allows
- where two parts look similar but are intentionally different, mark that before batching anything

## 3/4 in Plywood Sequence

### Full-size breakdown

1. Break down the three top substrate blanks first: `TOP-01A`, `TOP-01B`, `TOP-01C`.
2. Break down the six cabinet side and partition blanks next: `LM-01`, `LM-02`, `CM-01`, `RM-01`, `RM-02`.
3. Break down the remaining large panels: `LM-03`, `RM-03`, `RM-05`, `CM-06`, `ASM-01`.

### Repeated rip families

1. Rip the `4-1/2 in` family together where practical:
   - `LM-04`
   - `RM-04`
   - `TOP-04`
   - `RF-02`
2. Rip the `3-1/2 in` family together where practical:
   - `CM-03`
   - `CM-05`
   - `FT-03`
3. Rip the `2-1/2 in` family together where practical:
   - `TOP-03`
   - `ASM-04`
4. Rip the `1-3/4 in` family together where practical:
   - `RM-09`
   - `ASM-02`
   - `ASM-03`

### Do-not-batch warnings

- `LM-04` and `RM-04` look alike but are not the same final length
- `CM-02` and `CM-03` look alike but are not the same width
- `TOP-04` and `RF-02` share a rip family but not a final crosscut length

## 3/4 in Prefinished Plywood Sequence

1. Break down the three top wear skins first: `TOP-02A`, `TOP-02B`, `TOP-02C`.
2. Cut `MS-04` from the remaining offcut while the finished-face orientation is still clear.
3. Mark the finished face immediately on the tape or protective film, not on the actual finished surface.

## 1/2 in Plywood Sequence

1. Break down the drawer family as one session:
   - `DR-01` through `DR-07`
2. Cut `LM-05` and `RM-05` in the same material session.
3. Keep drawer side and drawer front/back families grouped by width so repeated setups stay together.

## Small-Part Batch Opportunities

- `MS-01` and `MS-04` share the same blank size
- `TOP-06` and `FT-02` share the same final `4 x 4 in` size
- `MS-05` and `MS-06` share the same blank and final size
- `FT-01` side plates are identical and should be cut as a pair

## Before Leaving The Saw

- confirm every part is labeled
- confirm handed or mirrored parts have their show face and inside face marked
- stack the mockup-gated parts separately so they do not get mistaken for cut-now parts
