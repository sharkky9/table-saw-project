# Sliding Carriage

## Purpose

The sliding carriage is the primary large-panel crosscut aid in this variant.

## Design Rules

- carriage rides parallel to the blade
- carriage parks with a positive stop and does not foul normal rip-fence use
- fence is removable and re-squareable
- guide hardware stays adjustable until the real fit is proven
- the current branch only claims about `12 in` of honest literal translation inside the fixed `48 in` core
- guide strips stay top-mounted and bridge above the left rail pockets instead of hanging hardware into them
- carriage top must finish flush to `0.000 to -0.005 in` relative to the fixed top

## Hardware Package

- `HDW-09` guide rail stock
- `HDW-10` wear pads and adjusters
- `HDW-12` fence track or extrusion
- `HDW-16` stop and park hardware
- `FAST-12`, `FAST-13`, and `FAST-16`

## Fit Sequence

1. Laminate `SC-01` and `SC-02`.
2. Fit temporary guide strips and temporary setup blocks first.
3. Tune glide and flush height before drilling any fence hardware.
4. Install `SC-05` only after the real guide spacing, rail-pocket bridge zones, and park-stop locations are proven.
5. Fit the selected adjustable pads or equivalent wear surfaces.
6. Install `SC-03` and `SC-04`.
7. Add park stops and travel limits.
8. Square the fence to the blade.

## Fence Adjustment

- One end of the fence should act as the fixed reference end.
- The opposite end must have a micro-adjust feature such as a push screw or slotted-bolt correction.
- Do not call the fence finished until it can be removed, reinstalled, and re-squared without recutting the carriage body.

## Flush-Tuning Method

1. Shim or adjust the guide strips until the carriage top is never proud of the fixed top.
2. Check flushness with a straightedge at the front, middle, and rear of the stroke.
3. Recheck after the stops are installed because stop hardware can preload the carriage.

## Acceptance

- carriage glides through the full `12 in` literal stroke without play or bind
- carriage travel stays within `0.005 in` of parallel to the blade over the full stroke
- carriage top is flush to the fixed top within `0.000 to -0.005 in`
- fence repeats square after removal
- parked carriage clears the saw cast top and ordinary rip-fence use
- stop hardware repeats travel and park positions
