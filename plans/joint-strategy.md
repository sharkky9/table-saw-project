# Joint Strategy

## Plinth

- glue + `3 in` structural screws
- predrill near ends
- keep rails square before adding caster reinforcement blocks

## Cabinet Carcasses

- cabinet screws or Confirmat-style screws for main plywood box assembly
- glue at all long-grain plywood joints
- install backs only after the carcass is confirmed square

## Module Joining

- clamp modules flush
- use washer-head screws through doubled walls
- recheck top-edge flushness before tightening all fasteners

## Saw Cradle

- ledgers screwed and glued to carcass sides
- slotted crossrails bolted
- jack screws provide fine adjustment
- saw-mount bolts drilled only after the survey and fit-up

## Sliding Carriage

- carriage panel glue-up uses full-spread glue and cauls or a flat reference surface
- runner backers stay adjustable until the carriage glides with low play
- fence extrusion and stop hardware are all mechanical and adjustable

## Left Support Table

- support-table panel glue-up uses full-spread glue and cauls or a flat reference surface
- hinge screws go in only after the support-table blank is square
- hard stops and the leg set the deployed geometry; the floor is not the primary precision datum

## Service Panels And Hatches

- router hatch may use simple latches or magnets
- front service face uses removable screws into repeatable cleats
- control subpanel must disconnect cleanly before the service face is removed
