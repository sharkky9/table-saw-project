# Saw Cradle

## Purpose

The cradle sets the stripped saw flush to the surrounding top and keeps that setting locked after the saw is bolted down.

## Architecture

- fixed side ledgers establish the broad mount-plane zone
- an oversized plywood cradle deck spans the ledgers and stays undrilled until first fit
- four `3/8-16` jack screws under the deck provide fine flush tuning
- saw bolts lock only after the deck is tuned flush and transfer-drilled from the real saw

## Hardware Package

- `FAST-07` saw-mount bolts washers and lock nuts
- `FAST-17` four jack screws with jam nuts
- `FAST-18` threaded inserts or tee nuts for the jack-screw pads or blocking

## Adjustment Sequence

1. Install the side ledgers at nominal mount-plane height.
2. Install the oversized cradle deck on the ledgers.
3. Install the jack screws so the deck has fine height control at all four corners.
4. Set the stripped saw on the deck.
5. Shift the saw until the blade datum, slot alignment, and carriage park gap agree with the modeled opening.
6. Tune height with the jack screws until the cast top is flush or slightly low.
7. Check all four cast-top corners plus both miter-slot transitions.
8. Tighten the jack-screw jam nuts.
9. Recheck flushness.
10. Transfer-mark or transfer-punch the mount holes from the real saw.
11. Drill the deck and install final saw-mount hardware only after the setting is proven.

## Acceptance Tolerance

- saw top flushness: `0.000 to -0.005 in`
- saw top must never finish above the surrounding top

## Measurement Inputs Required Before Final Drilling

- foot-pad footprint
- stripped blade `y` datum
- rail envelope and overhang measurements that affect the opening and park gap
- lowest underside protrusion

Helpful but not mandatory before tuning:

- one mount-hole reference such as `front_left_mount_hole_x`
- mount-hole diameter if you are buying exact hardware before transfer drilling

Exact foot-center mapping is intentionally not required. The real saw is the drilling template for the cradle deck.
