# Assembly-Mode Strategy

## Recommendation

Do not convert the permanent top into a full-time clamping grid on day one. That would compromise the saw-support surface too early.

Use a layered approach instead.

## Layer 1: Fixed Features In The Permanent Top

Install two flush aluminum T-tracks in the far-left field:

- Track 1 centerline: `8 in` from the left edge
- Track 2 centerline: `20 in` from the left edge
- Track run: from `y = 6 in` to `y = 42 in`

Why this location:

- it stays entirely left of the saw opening
- it does not interfere with right-side rail travel
- it gives real clamp and stop locations for glue-ups, sanding jigs, and temporary fixtures

## Layer 2: Use The Bench Surface Itself

For ordinary assembly:

- lower the blade fully
- install a blank or solid throat plate
- remove the router fence
- use the bench as a large flat surface

This is already useful without turning the entire top into a fixture plate.

## Layer 3: Future Removable Overlay

Plan a `60 x 36 x 3/4 in` removable overlay that:

- registers to the left and rear edges
- occupies the rear `36 in` of the bench depth, not the front-wing zone
- anchors with four star knobs into threaded inserts
- starts with two additional T-tracks
- can later be perforated with `20 mm` holes if you actually want MFT behavior
- carries an underside stiffener where it spans the saw opening so clamp loads do not sag the panel into the throat area

## What The Overlay Should Solve

- more clamp points than the fixed bench should permanently carry
- sacrificial track-saw work
- glue-up work where squeeze-out is expected
- experimentation with dogs and fences without risking the permanent top

## What Not To Do

- Do not place permanent T-track in the main right-side support field.
- Do not put proud hardware where sheet goods need to slide.
- Do not route the permanent top for a full dog-hole grid until you have used the bench enough to know you want it.
- Do not assume the overlay can float unsupported over the saw opening; it needs either the lowered saw top beneath it or its dedicated stiffener.
