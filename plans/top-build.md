# Top Build

## Purpose

This section covers the two-layer fixed top build before any precision openings are machined.

The goal is a flat, durable `1-1/2 in` top that is easy to build in a real shop:

- `TOP-01*` plywood substrate carries the structure
- `TOP-02*` prefinished plywood wear skin provides the working surface
- openings are machined only after the laminated pairs are cured, attached, and field-fit gates are open

## Shop Assumptions

- track saw or circular saw with a straightedge guide is strongly preferred for the `90 in` blanks
- at least four `48 in` class clamps are needed for the rear panel glue-up
- a second person is strongly recommended for the rear `90 x 33 in` lamination
- glue the raw back of the wear skin to the raw face of the substrate; do not try to bond to the prefinished face

## Hardware And Consumables

- `HDW-06` Type II PVA glue
- `FAST-03` washer-head screws for top-to-carcass attachment from below
- cauls wrapped in packing tape or wax paper so squeeze-out does not glue them to the panel
- painter's tape for finished-face protection near clamp pads if needed

## Lamination Sequence

1. Dry-fit each substrate and wear-skin pair on a known-flat surface.
2. Check every blank for bow or twist before glue. If a blank shows more than about `1/8 in` of bow over its length, replace it or flatten it before continuing.
3. Mark a common front edge and left edge on each pair so the parts go back together the same way after glue spread.
4. Sand or scuff only the raw glue face if needed. Do not sand through the prefinished top face.
5. Spread `HDW-06` on the raw substrate face with a roller or spreader. Aim for full coverage, not puddles.
6. Drop the wear skin in place with the finished face up and the raw face down.
7. Clamp from the center outward with cauls so pressure is distributed across the full panel. For the rear panel, treat this as a two-person glue-up.
8. Clean squeeze-out from exposed edges before it hardens.
9. Let the laminated panel cure flat before moving it to machining or carcass install.

## Top Installation Sequence

1. Dry-fit the laminated top panels on the joined carcass before fastening anything.
2. Confirm the front/rear split still lands at the intended `y = 15 in` seam and the rear panel carries the saw-opening front shoulder.
3. Install the seam cleats and local blocking first.
4. Attach the top from below with `FAST-03` washer-head screws through clearance holes in the stretchers and cleats.
5. Do not glue the top to the carcass. The top must stay removable enough for rework if the field-fit steps force local changes.
6. Keep screws out of future opening zones, router-plate ledgers, and any known trim paths.

## Quality Checks

- laminated pairs stay flat after cure
- finished faces stay clean and uncrushed at clamp pads
- all three top sections register to the same front reference line before fastening
- seam cleats land fully under the panel joints
- top sits on the carcass without rocking before screws are tightened

## Attachment Rules

- use screws from below, not glue, for top-to-carcass attachment
- drill clearance holes in the driving member so the screw actually pulls the top down
- elongate holes slightly across the grain if local seasonal movement insurance is desired
- keep fastener heads accessible from below where service or rework may still be needed
