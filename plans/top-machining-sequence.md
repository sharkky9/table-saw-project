# Top Machining Sequence

## Hard Gate

Do not start this sequence until:

- the stripped-saw survey is complete
- the saw cradle is tuned flush
- the support table and support drawer have passed fit-up proof

## Tools And Bits

- straight bit sized for your guide setup
- pattern or flush-trim bit
- guide bushing or template guide if using templates
- chamfer or easing bit for slot-end treatment

## Sequence

1. Mark the final saw opening from the actual saw fit.
2. Cut the saw opening first.
3. Cut the router lift recess from the top face.
4. Cut the router through-opening.
5. Machine fixed front and rear miter-track recesses.
6. Machine carriage guide-strip zones and park-stop locations only after the support table is proven.
7. Machine underside right-side reliefs that are required by the real rail sweep.

## Acceptance Checks

- saw opening gap matches the real fit target
- no proud edge around the saw opening
- router plate sits flush with no rocking
- front and rear track extensions align to the saw slots
- carriage guide zones are cut only after the support package is proven
- no cut intrudes into the rail keep-clear lanes
